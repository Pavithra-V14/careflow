# For the clinician (Priya)

> **AI-prepared, not approved by Priya.** Not clinical advice. Never loaded by the application; never marked approved.

**Read (about 45 minutes):** `clinician-options.md` (items 23 to 34, each with two or three options, the trade-offs, the safe default and one suggested answer), then `docs/design/clinician-sheet.md` for the original wording of the questions.

**Decide (about 30 to 45 minutes, can be split over days):**
- Items 23, 24, 25, 26, 30, 32, 33: pick an option per item (A, B or C) or write your own. These are who-does-what and how-fast choices.
- Items 27, 28, 29, 31 and 34: these need your own clinical content (recurrence rules, plan conditions, readiness questions, result codes, encounter and minor/pregnancy handling). The sheet gives only a structure and questions; the content must come from you. Write "not yet" where you cannot answer; the software then keeps sending that case to a person.
- Anything touching red-flag rules or triage text is yours alone to write and needs two named people to approve.

**How to answer:** write the answer, your name and the date in the answer column of `docs/design/clinician-sheet.md`, or sign the last table in `clinician-options.md`. Nothing is live until a named person signs and it is reviewed.

**Total: about 1.5 to 2 hours**, a rough estimate (item 29 and 27 can take longer if you write the content now).
